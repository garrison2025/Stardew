# Standard Community Center tracker
30 bundles in 6 rooms, plus independent post-center Missing Bundle. Independently encoded item names, quantity, quality, required slot counts and rewards checked against https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Bundles and https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Modding:Bundles . Rules include 3/4 quality crops, 5/6 animal, 6/12 artisan, 5/9 exotic, 5/10 crab pot, 2/4 adventurer, 5/6 Missing Bundle, and two separate 99-wood stacks. Gold purchases only complete when marked fully paid. Counts are per donation slot, not an inventory optimizer; any higher quality is accepted conceptually, user records qualifying donated quantities. Standard progress excludes Missing Bundle. Seasonal tags are common-route reminders, not exact availability; source hints deliberately short and non-exhaustive. No game art or extracted assets. Private manual local persistence/import, public export, legacy custom list retained. No remixed or modded mode, save donation extraction, exact patch fixtures, automatic rewards, cloud synchronization or cross-tab CAS for this key yet. No new routes, indexing, rights-gate or advertising changes.
