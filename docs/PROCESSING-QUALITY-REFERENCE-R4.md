Compared 25 individual crop phase sequences to stardew.gs (build 1.6.15.24356; site reports direct game-data extraction; https://stardew.gs/about/). 24 matched before correction; broccoli was incorrectly [2,2,2,2] rather than [1,2,2,3] (https://stardew.gs/crops/broccoli/). Green bean and hops use five positive phases in the current source, not the legacy wiki module's display-only extra zero stage. Exact stage sequence is asserted for the 25 checked crops; this is not yet full independent fertilizer-calendar parity. Rice Shoot shop availability starts in year 2, confirmed by https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Rice_Shoot and https://stardew.gs/crops/unmilled-rice/. Owned seed scenario accepts all catalogued crops and requires explicit stock; this permits rice found in year one without implying unlimited purchases.

The room calculator now offers continuous reload (upper bound), morning-only 06:00 collection, or four scheduled visits at 06:00/12:00/18:00/00:00. Machine time uses 1600 effective minutes per day, 1200 between 06:00 and 02:00 plus 400 overnight, from https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Preserves_Jar. A completed batch must be collected during a scheduled visit before refilling. No game ticks, farm walking, interruption or actual save replay simulated. Daily economy UI defaults to 1600 while retaining explicit custom day length and once-daily morning collection.
