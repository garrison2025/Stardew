# Rights clearance — 2026-11-28

## Evidence and limits
- https://stardew.info/ : public V3 planner credits all game assets to ConcernedApe and offers advertising / Patreon ad removal. No publicly documented transferable license was found. Do not infer permission from operation.
- https://upload.farm/about : site explicitly reports permission from ConcernedApe and Chucklefish. This is a useful precedent, not a grant to Field & Farm.
- https://forums.stardewvalley.net/threads/is-it-allowed-to-use-game-assets-for-helpers.4107/ : Jan 4 2021 moderator Odin says public helper sites generally credit game assets. No explicit ad-supported grant.
- https://forums.stardewvalley.net/threads/stardew-valley-art-asset-usage-scope.53469/ : July 12 2026 moderator recommends asking ConcernedApe directly.
- https://www.stardewvalley.net/contact/ : official inquiry channel. https://www.stardewvalley.net/faq/ covers monetized YouTube videos, not a blanket website grant.
- https://www.copyright.gov/help/faq/faq-protect.html : facts and methods are not copyrightable as such, but original expression and compilations may be. Do not assume every fictional game dataset is automatically an unprotected fact.
- https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Stardew_Valley_Wiki:Copyrights : wiki CC BY-NC-SA is not permission to reproduce wiki expression in an ad-supported product.
- Starplan pinned 7ee52554a2e48f47a9d99e256830d7b1b2e5954f: AGPL code; game artwork expressly excluded from that license and used non-commercially.

## Separate approval scopes
1. Independent art and Kenney CC0 decorative assets: existing provenance, no game asset grant needed for those original assets.
2. Functional numeric rules and independently written code: document provenance, distinguish individual facts and mechanics from protected compilations and fictional expressive content.
3. Complete terrain reference flags and initial coordinates derived from Starplan: commercial use not established. No game map imagery bundled. Preview stays noindex/ad-free; approval required before monetization of this derived pack, or remove it from that release.
4. Original game map backgrounds, building/item sprites: absent from current release; only integrate after confirmed permission with its conditions.
5. Brand: identify compatibility nominatively, retain independent brand and clear disclaimer; no official logos or claim of affiliation.

## Release decision
No approval is inferred from silence, attribution, noncommercial use or competitor practice. If permission is granted, record scope and restrictions, implement only permitted assets, retain credits, and independently review upstream rights. Otherwise continue independently drawn maps/functional rules within appropriate scope or remove disputed derived packs for the commercial release. Global release gates and ads remain unchanged. This is project evidence and risk management, not legal advice.
